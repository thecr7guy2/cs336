Mid-session capture: **$ARGUMENTS**

**This must not break their flow.** Append to today's journal, reply in one or
two lines, stop. No analysis, no teaching, no follow-up questions unless they
asked one.

Classify and file it:

- **confusion** → `## Where I got stuck` in today's journal, *and* a 🔴 entry in
  `questions/open.md`. Capture what they believed at the moment of confusion,
  not a tidy statement of the problem.
- **click** → `## The click`, in their exact words. Never paraphrase a click;
  the phrasing is the artifact.
- **bug** → `## Where I got stuck` with the four fields (thought / symptom /
  actual / what broke the deadlock). A fixed bug is a located misconception —
  this is the highest-value thing in the session, so get the "I thought" right.

If it involved genuine struggle, tag it `#seed`.

If they haven't run `/start` today, create today's journal from the template
first and note that the session started mid-stream.
