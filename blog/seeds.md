# Blog seeds

Harvested weekly from journal entries tagged `#seed`. **Never write these
retroactively** — once you understand something you permanently lose access to
what it felt like not to, and that feeling is the whole product.

A good seed has: what I believed → what the symptom was → what resolved it.
A bad seed is "explain how X works" — nobody needs another one of those.

**Ripeness:** 🌱 raw moment · 🌿 has a shape · 🌳 ready to draft
